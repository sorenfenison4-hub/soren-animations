TITLE
What if you could clone yourself like a starfish?

DESCRIPTION
Can a starfish clone itself?

Yes. A few kinds of starfish copy themselves by tearing their own body in two. The two sides pull away from each other until the middle splits, and each half grows back the arms it is missing. Both new starfish are clones of the first one.

It takes a while. In one species raised in a lab, both halves had regrown their arms about a month after the split. Its body is built in repeating parts around the middle: even its stomach reaches into every arm through a pair of digestive glands.

People can't do this. A lost arm never grows back, so half a person would stay half a person.
