TITLE
What if you could breathe like a shark?

DESCRIPTION
Do sharks die if they stop swimming?

Some do. A great white shark can't pump water over its gills the way most fish can. It has to keep swimming with its mouth open, so water rushes over its gills and brings it oxygen. If it stopped, it would suffocate.

Not every shark works like this. Nurse sharks and other sharks that rest on the sea floor can pump water over their gills while lying still. Great whites and makos can't.

And great whites really do keep moving. One tagged great white swam from South Africa to Australia and back, about 20,000 kilometers, in under nine months. With a shark's gills, you could never stop swimming, not even to sleep.

Please never practice holding your breath in water: people can black out without any warning.
