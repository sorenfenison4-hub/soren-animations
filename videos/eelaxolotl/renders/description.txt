TITLE:
What if you had an eel's shock or an axolotl's healing?

DESCRIPTION:
Can an axolotl really regrow its legs?

Yes. An axolotl can regrow a lost leg, its tail, its spinal cord and even parts of its heart and brain, and it can regrow the same leg again and again without a scar. Here she gets that power for life: broken legs, stunt falls and old knees are never a problem.

He gets the electric eel's power instead. The strongest electric eel, named as a new species in 2019, can put out about 860 volts. It makes that shock with thousands of special cells stacked inside its body like the cells in a battery. Here he uses it to light birthday candles, jump-start cars and skip the power bill.

So which one wins: eel shock or axolotl healing?
