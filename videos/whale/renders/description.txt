TITLE
What if a whale swallowed you like Jonah?

DESCRIPTION
Can a whale swallow a human?

Almost never. Most whales simply can't fit you down. A blue whale is the biggest animal on Earth, but its throat is only about as wide as a grapefruit, because it lives on tiny krill.

The only whale with a throat big enough is a very large sperm whale, around 18 meters long. Even then there would be no air inside, and the first of its four stomach chambers has thick muscular walls that squeeze its food.

In 2021 a humpback whale off Cape Cod caught a lobster diver in its mouth by accident. It couldn't swallow him, and after about 30 to 40 seconds it came up and spat him back out. He was bruised but alive.
