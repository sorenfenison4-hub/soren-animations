TITLE
What if you could squeeze like an octopus?

DESCRIPTION
How can an octopus squeeze through tiny holes?

An octopus has no bones at all. The only hard part of its body is its beak, which is made of a tough material a bit like your fingernails. If the beak fits through a gap, the rest of the soft body can follow. A fully grown giant Pacific octopus can squeeze through a hole about the size of a lemon.

You have 206 bones holding you up. Your muscles pull on them to stand, sit and lift your arms. Without them, there would be nothing to pull on, and you would just flop.
