TITLE:
What if you could regrow your arm like a lizard?

DESCRIPTION:
Do lizard tails grow back?

Yes. A lizard can drop its tail on purpose when something grabs it. The tail breaks at weak spots built into its tail bones, and the piece left behind keeps wriggling, so the predator goes after that instead.

But the tail that grows back is different. The old one had a row of little bones. The new one has none, just one long tube of cartilage, the same stuff as the tip of your nose.

Your arm has 30 bones: one in your upper arm, two in your forearm and 27 in your hand. Grow it back like a lizard's tail and all of them would be one tube. No elbow, no wrist, no knuckles.
