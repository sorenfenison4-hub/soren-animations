TITLE
What if you could swing like a gibbon?

DESCRIPTION
Do gibbons ever fall out of trees?

Gibbons are the best swingers on Earth. They hang from their long arms, and their wrist works like a ball joint, so the body turns under each branch while the hand holds on.

But even gibbons miss. When scientists studied the skeletons of wild gibbons, about one in three had old broken bones that had healed, most likely from falls.

A gibbon weighs only about 6 kilos. You weigh about ten times more, so the same fall would hit you a lot harder. Keep the swinging low and over soft ground.
