TITLE
What if you painted a cow like a zebra?

DESCRIPTION
He painted his cow with black and white stripes, and the biting flies stopped landing on her. Biting flies don't just itch. They can spread disease.

Stripes mess with a fly's eyes. Filmed up close, flies zoom in at a striped body without slowing down and bump right off. On real zebras, flies land less than a quarter as often as on horses. Scientists even painted stripes on cows, and far fewer flies landed on them.

So that's why zebras have stripes. Now every cow on the farm wants a paint job.
