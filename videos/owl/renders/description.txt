TITLE
What if you had an owl's eyes?

DESCRIPTION
Why can't owls move their eyes?

Owl eyes aren't balls. They're long tubes, held in place by a ring of small bone plates, so they can't roll around in the skull. To look at something, an owl has to turn its whole head.

An owl can turn its head about 270 degrees. It has 14 neck bones, and you have 7. Its arteries also run through bone openings much wider than they are, and it has spare blood pools under its jaw, so blood keeps reaching its brain while it turns.

Your neck isn't built for that. Twisting it hard can tear an artery that feeds your brain, so never force your neck further than it wants to go.
