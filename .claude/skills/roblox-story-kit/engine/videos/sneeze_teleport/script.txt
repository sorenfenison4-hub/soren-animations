# sneeze_teleport - "In this world, every time you sneeze, you teleport somewhere random."
# Formula: hook (result first: kid flying a plane) -> rule -> 3 society examples -> "But" pivot ->
# escalation ladder (club, grandma, space, back to class, cockpit, pilot gone, whole plane sneezing) ->
# "Then I noticed something" (anything you touch teleports with you) -> payoff -> button (callback to the uncle).
I sneezed in class, and teleported into the cockpit of a flying plane! Then the pilot sneezed too! He vanished, and now I was flying the plane!
You see, in this world, every time you sneeze, you teleport somewhere random. That's why pepper is illegal. Florists wear space suits. And my uncle sneezed during his driving test, and landed on the moon. He still failed.
But I'm allergic to everything. And yesterday, my teacher, Miss Whiskers, brought her cat to class.
The cat jumped on my desk. My nose started tingling. Ah... ah... CHOO! Poof! I landed on stage at a comedy club. Two hundred people stared at me. Someone threw a tomato. Ah... CHOO! Poof! I landed on my grandma's couch. She screamed and smacked me with the remote. Ah... CHOO! I was floating in space! I couldn't breathe! Ah... CHOO! Poof! I was back in class. And the cat was sitting on my head.
Ah... ah... CHOO! Poof! Now I was in the cockpit of a plane, thirty thousand feet in the air. The pilot looked at me. Then he looked at the cat. I'm allergic to cats! Ah... CHOO! Poof! He was gone. I was flying the plane! The co-pilot sneezed. Poof! Gone. Then two hundred passengers started sneezing. Poof! Poof! Poof! Beep, beep, beep! We were going down!
Then I noticed something. Every time I teleported, the cat came with me. Because when you sneeze, anything you're touching comes too. So I grabbed the steering wheel, hugged the cat, and sneezed the biggest sneeze of my life. Ah... ah... CHOO! Poof! The entire plane teleported. It landed in the school parking lot. Perfectly parked. Everybody cheered.
Miss Whiskers ran outside and hugged me. You saved my cat! Then the cat sneezed. Poof! Now we were all on the moon. Next to my uncle.
