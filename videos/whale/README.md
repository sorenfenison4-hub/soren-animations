# What if a whale swallowed you like Jonah? (22.3 s)

Bible lane test. Frame 0: the guy in a humpback's closing jaws ("TOO BIG TO SWALLOW"); stuck; blue whale throat vs a
grapefruit; an 18 m sperm whale gulps him; inside: no air, stomach 1 of 4 squeezes; 2021 humpback spits the diver out
and the loop puts him back in the mouth. All whales are code-drawn (`Humpback` / `HumpbackJaws` with hinged jaws,
`SpermWhale` with a dropping jaw, a stomach blob that squeezes) in `src/Whale.tsx` - reusable for any sea / swallowed
story. No Higgsfield pictures; library poses only.
