TITLE
What if you could land like a cat?

DESCRIPTION
Do cats always land on their feet?

A falling cat turns itself over in midair. Its inner ear tells it which way is down, it twists its front half first, then its back half, and it comes down on its paws.

Vets in New York looked at 132 cats that had fallen from high-rise windows, and about 9 in 10 survived. But landing on your feet doesn't stop the rest of you. The legs give, the chin hits the ground, and more than half of those cats hurt their face. About one in six cracked the roof of the mouth.

So if you could land like a cat, you'd still smash your chin. Cats don't really have nine lives: keep window screens closed, and don't jump from heights yourself.
