TITLE:
What if you could jump like a flea?

DESCRIPTION:
How high can a flea jump?

A cat flea is only 1 to 3 millimetres long, but in lab tests it jumped up to 17 centimetres high and 48 centimetres far. For something your size, that is like jumping clean over a 30-storey tower. A kangaroo, which is about your size, has been seen clearing a fence 2.4 metres high.

A flea does not jump with muscle alone. It squeezes a pad of a rubbery protein called resilin inside its body, like a spring, and then lets go. It works like a catapult.

The problem is coming down. A flea is so light for its size that air slows its fall, so it lands fine. A person falling 30 storeys would not survive. Please don't jump from anything high: even a fall of a few metres can badly hurt a person.
