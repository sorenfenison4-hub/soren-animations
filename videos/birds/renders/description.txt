TITLE
Why don't birds on power lines get shocked?

DESCRIPTION
This bird is sitting on a wire with thousands of volts in it, so why isn't it fried? Both of its feet are on the same wire, so they're at the same voltage. No voltage difference means no current goes through the bird, and the electricity just stays in the wire.

But if a wing touches a second wire, there's suddenly a path right through the bird. That's why big birds with wide wings, like eagles, are the ones most at risk.

Never touch or go near a power line yourself. You're standing on the ground, so you would become the path.
