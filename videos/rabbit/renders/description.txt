TITLE
What if you had a rabbit's eyes?

DESCRIPTION
Can rabbits see behind them?

Almost. A rabbit's eyes sit on the sides of its head, so together they see nearly all the way around it, even behind and above. That helps it spot a hawk or a fox sneaking up from any side. Your eyes look forward and cover only about half of that.

The catch is a small blind spot right in front of its nose and under its chin. A rabbit can't see food that is right under its nose, so it finds it by sniffing and by feeling with its whiskers.

With eyes like that, you could see what is behind you, but not what is right in front of your face.
