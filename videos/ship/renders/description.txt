TITLE
Why does a steel ship float?

DESCRIPTION
This block of steel sinks straight to the bottom, but a huge steel ship floats. The difference is the shape. A ship's hull is hollow, so it's mostly air inside.

As the ship settles into the water, it pushes water out of the way. Once the water it has pushed aside weighs as much as the ship, the water pushes up just as hard as the ship pushes down, so it floats.

A solid block can't push aside that much water. Steel is about eight times heavier than the same amount of water, so the block sinks.
