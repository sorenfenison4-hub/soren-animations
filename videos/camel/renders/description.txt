What if you could drink like a camel?

DESCRIPTION
How much water can a camel drink at once?

A camel can go about twenty days without a drink. When it finally drinks, it can gulp up to 110 litres of water in around ten minutes. Its oval red blood cells can swell to 240 percent of their size without bursting. The red cells of most other animals burst at about 150 percent.

Your kidneys can only get rid of about one litre of water an hour. Drink far more than that, fast, and the extra water thins the salt in your blood, moves into your cells and makes them swell. In your brain that is dangerous. Doctors call it water intoxication.

And the hump? It holds fat, not water.

Please don't try to drink a lot of water quickly. Drink when you are thirsty, and get medical help if you feel sick, confused or get a bad headache after drinking a lot.
