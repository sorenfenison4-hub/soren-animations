TITLE:
What if you had a fly's eyes?

DESCRIPTION:
Why can't you swat a fly?

A fly sees about four times faster than you do, so your hand coming down looks slow to it. Before it jumps, it works out where the threat is coming from and sets its legs to leap the other way, all in about a tenth of a second.

The catch is how sharp it sees. Each fly eye is made of more than three thousand tiny eyes, and each one sees just one dot of the picture. Put together, that is a blurry mosaic, so with a fly's eyes you would be almost blind.

Eyes built that way only get sharper by getting bigger. To see as sharp as you do now, each one would have to be about a meter wide.
