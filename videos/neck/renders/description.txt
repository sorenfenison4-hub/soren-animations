TITLE
What if you woke up with a giraffe's neck?

DESCRIPTION
Why don't giraffes faint?

If you woke up with a giraffe's neck, you'd stand up and pass out. Your head would be almost two meters above your heart, and a human heart just isn't strong enough to push blood that high.

A giraffe's heart pumps at about double your blood pressure, so blood still reaches its brain way up there. All that pressure would make its legs swell, but a tight wrapping of tissue in its lower legs helps hold the fluid in.

So you'd need a whole new heart. Or you could just lie down.
