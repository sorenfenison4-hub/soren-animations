TITLE
Why can't you sleep the first night in a hotel?

DESCRIPTION
The bed is perfect, but on his first night in a new hotel, every little sound wakes him up. The fridge, footsteps in the hallway, the ice machine.

It's not the bed. It's his brain. On the first night somewhere new, the left half of your brain sleeps lighter than the right. It reacts more to strange sounds and wakes you up faster, like a night guard. Scientists call it the first-night effect, and by the second night it's gone. Dolphins go even further: they sleep with one half of the brain at a time.

So by night two he sleeps fine. Except tomorrow, he moves to a new hotel.
