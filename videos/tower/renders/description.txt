TITLE
Why is he building a tower to the Moon?

DESCRIPTION
He's been adding to his tower every year for sixty years, and it's only about as tall as a door. That's on purpose. He isn't trying to reach the Moon.

The Moon drifts away from Earth about 3.8 centimeters a year. Scientists measure it by bouncing lasers off mirrors the Apollo astronauts left on the Moon. So every year, he makes his tower exactly 3.8 centimeters taller.

After sixty years, the Moon is about 2.3 meters farther away, and he's 2.3 meters higher. So they're exactly as close as the night they met.
