TITLE
Why should you never whisper in a round room?

DESCRIPTION
The king's advisor leaned on the wall of a round hall and whispered his plan. Forty meters away, the sweeper heard every word. Nobody believed him, until he put the king's ear to the far wall.

In a round room, a whisper doesn't spread out into the room. It hugs the curved wall and slides all the way around, so someone on the other side can hear it clearly. These are called whispering galleries, and the physicist Lord Rayleigh explained how they work in the 1870s.

The most famous one is the Whispering Gallery in St Paul's Cathedral in London. Whisper against the wall and you can be heard on the other side of the dome, about 34 meters away.
