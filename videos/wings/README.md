# What if you woke up with wings? (22.9 s)

Story Short, no facts. Frame 0: the guy, upright and big, brown bird wings spread, just bonked into his bedroom ceiling
("REAL WINGS. LOW CEILING."), birds already watching through the window. Falls on the bed, BONK again; outside he
takes off past roofs, birds and clouds; birds follow him in a V, land with him, sit with him on a power line and bow
("LEADER"). Spoken like ask: "Tap like, and you'll get a bird too" - a like sticker's thumb sprouts wings and turns into
a blue bird on his head; dawn: birds tap on his window, he shoots out of bed and the loop puts him back at the ceiling.

Everything new is code in `src/Wings.tsx`, reusable: `WingPair` / `WingGuy` (feathered wings behind any library pose,
flap angle + spread), `Bird` (side-view doodle bird: perched / flying / bow / landing squash / thumb mark), `BirdFront`
(flying toward the camera), `Feathers` (loose feathers drifting down), a bedroom (window with birds, bed, blanket lump
that flies off), a power line that sags under each bird, the like-sticker -> winged thumb gag. No Higgsfield pictures.
The usual three-sticker CallToAction is left out on purpose: the voice asks for the like as part of the story.
