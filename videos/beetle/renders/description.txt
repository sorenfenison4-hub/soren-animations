TITLE:
What if you had a dung beetle's strength?

DESCRIPTION:
How strong is a dung beetle?

The bull-headed dung beetle is only about 1 centimetre long, but in one experiment the strongest male could pull 1,141 times its own body weight. For a 70 kg person, that would be about 80 tonnes, roughly the weight of 13 full-grown male elephants.

The beetle gets away with it because it is tiny. Muscle strength depends on how thick a muscle is, while body weight depends on the whole volume of the body, so the smaller an animal is, the stronger it is for its size. At your size, the same strength would need a skeleton that could take 80 tonnes.

Yours can't. Your spine is a stack of 24 small bones built to carry you, and a load like that would crush it long before you got it off the ground. In real life, never lift more than you can handle, and lift with your legs, not your back.
