TITLE
What if you lived as long as these animals?

DESCRIPTION
Which animal lives the longest?

A giant tortoise named Jonathan was about 190 years old in 2022, the oldest known land animal. One Greenland shark was estimated at around 400 years old, and an ocean quahog clam named Ming lived to 507.

The immortal jellyfish beats all of them. When it gets old, hurt or starved, it doesn't have to die: it shrinks into a little blob, settles on the sea floor and grows back into its baby stage, a polyp. Then it grows up again. It can still be eaten or get sick, so it isn't truly unkillable.

Living like the immortal jellyfish would mean turning back into a baby and starting over, again and again.
