TITLE
What if you had a bat's wings?

DESCRIPTION
What are bat wings made of?

A bat's wing is a hand. Its arm and four very long fingers hold up a thin sheet of skin, just two layers thick, stretched between them. Only the thumb stays free, with a small claw for climbing.

Your hand has the same bones, only much shorter. Grow bat wings, and your fingers would become the frame of the wings. You would keep two of your ten fingers free: your thumbs. No phone, no pizza, no high fives.

Bats are the only mammals that can truly fly. Most of them rest hanging upside down by their feet, so they can take off just by letting go.
