TITLE
What if you could run as fast as these animals?

DESCRIPTION
Is a tiger beetle faster than a cheetah?

An ostrich can run about 70 km/h (around 43 mph), faster than the fastest human ever, who peaked at about 44 km/h. A cheetah is faster still: the top speed measured on wild cheetahs wearing tracking collars was 93 km/h (58 mph), as fast as a car on an open road.

But for its size, a tiger beetle beats them both. The fastest one runs about 120 times its own body length every second, while a cheetah manages about 20. If you could run 120 of your body lengths a second, you'd be going about 750 km/h (470 mph), almost as fast as a passenger jet.

There's a catch. A tiger beetle runs so fast that its eyes can't take in enough light to make a picture, so it stops for a split second to look, then runs on. While it runs, it holds its antennae out in front, just above the ground, and feels its way around anything in its path. Without them, it runs straight into things.
