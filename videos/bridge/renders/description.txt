TITLE
Why does his bridge get longer every year?

DESCRIPTION
His little bridge is only about a meter long, but it joins two continents. His house sits on the North American plate, and his neighbor's sits on the Eurasian plate.

Iceland sits right on the Mid-Atlantic Ridge, where those two plates meet, and they pull apart about two centimeters a year. So every year, he adds a two centimeter plank. After fifty years, that's one meter.

And every morning, they still meet in the middle for coffee.
