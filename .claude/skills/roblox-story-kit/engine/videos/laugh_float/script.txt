# laugh_float - "In this world, laughing makes you float"
# Formula: hook (result first) -> rule -> 3 society examples -> "But" pivot -> escalation ladder
# (one kid, the class, the whole town) -> "Then I noticed something" (rule-based save) -> button.
I laughed at one joke in class, and floated straight out of the school window! Then my best friend laughed too! He flew right after me!
You see, in this world, laughing makes you lighter than air. The harder you laugh, the higher you float.
That's why comedy clubs have nets on the ceiling. Pilots aren't allowed to watch cartoons. And my grandma wears ankle weights to watch her favorite show.
But I have a problem. I laugh at everything. And yesterday, my school had its first ever Joke Day.
Our teacher, Mr. Pickle, told the first joke. Why was the math book sad? It had too many problems! Nobody laughed. Except me. I snorted, and my feet lifted off the floor!
I grabbed my desk, but my best friend Leo saw my face and burst out laughing. He shot straight up and bonked his head on the ceiling! Bonk!
That made the whole class laugh. Thirty kids floated up like balloons. Backpacks, pencils, and Mr. Pickle's toupee went flying everywhere.
Then the window popped open. Whoosh! The wind sucked us all outside. We floated over the playground, over the school, over the entire town.
Leo was laughing even harder. Stop laughing! I can't! It tickles up here! A pigeon landed on his head, and he giggled so hard he shot into a cloud.
Down below, the whole town was watching. And they thought it was hilarious. Big mistake. A thousand people floated up at once. Cars, dogs, even the mayor! The entire town was drifting into the sky.
There was only one person still on the ground. Grumpy Gus. The only kid in history who had never laughed. Not once. He was so heavy, his shoes left craters in the sidewalk.
Then I noticed something. Gus was the heaviest kid in the world. So I grabbed the mayor's tie. Leo grabbed my shoe. A thousand people grabbed each other. We made the longest human chain ever. Gus! Catch!
He caught the end and pulled. Hard. We all came down like a giant kite.
Everybody landed safely. The whole town cheered for Gus. And for the first time in his life, Gus smiled. Then he laughed. Uh oh. Whoosh! Gus rocketed straight into space. Somebody tell him something sad!
