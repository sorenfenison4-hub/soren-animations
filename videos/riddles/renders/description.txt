TITLE: 3 animal riddles: can you guess all three?

Which animal has three hearts and blue blood?

Riddle one is the octopus. One heart pumps blood around its body and two pump it through its gills. Its blood carries oxygen with copper instead of iron, which makes it blue.

Riddle two is the butterfly. It has taste sensors on its feet, so it lands on a leaf to taste it first. Females use this to find the right plant to lay their eggs on.

Riddle three is the wombat. Its gut squeezes its poop into cubes, and it leaves them on rocks and logs to mark its turf.
