What if you had a gecko's feet?

DESCRIPTION
How do geckos stick to walls?

A gecko's toes are covered in about six and a half million tiny hairs, and each one splits into even finer tips. They get so close to the wall that weak attraction between molecules holds them there. On paper, all of them together could hold about 130 kilograms.

So why can't you climb with gecko feet? Your weight grows much faster than the skin on your hands and feet as a body gets bigger. Scientists estimate a person would need sticky pads on about 40 percent of their body to walk up a wall, which is roughly their whole front.

Please don't try climbing walls without real safety gear.
