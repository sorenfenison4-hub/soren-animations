TITLE: What if you had a dolphin's blowhole?

How do dolphins sleep without drowning?

You breathe without thinking, even in your sleep. A dolphin can't. Its blowhole stays shut until it decides to open it, so every breath is a choice it has to make.

That's why a dolphin sleeps with only half its brain at a time. One half rests while the other stays awake to surface and breathe, and the eye on the awake side often stays open. The two halves take turns. In one study, dolphins kept watch and kept working for 15 days straight with no sign of getting tired.

Your brain doesn't work that way. When you fall asleep, both halves sleep, and your breathing runs on autopilot. With a blowhole like a dolphin's, nothing would open it once you dozed off.
